# Screenshots Required for AppSource

AppSource requires **at least 1 screenshot** (recommended up to 5).
Screenshots must be **1366x768 px** (PNG or JPG).

Take these screenshots with the plugin loaded in Word Desktop:

---

## Screenshot 1: "Select & Ask" (REQUIRED)
- Open a legal document (contract or clause) in Word
- Highlight a paragraph of text
- Show the AI:ssociate panel open on the right with a question typed
- **Caption**: "Highlight text in your document and ask AI:ssociate for legal analysis"

## Screenshot 2: "AI Response with Apply/Dismiss"
- Show a completed AI response in the chat panel
- Make sure the "Apply" and "Dismiss" buttons are visible
- **Caption**: "Review AI suggestions and apply them to your document with one click"

## Screenshot 3: "Legal Area Filters"
- Show the bottom of the panel with the legal area and scope dropdowns open
- **Caption**: "Filter by legal area and Austrian jurisdiction for targeted analysis"

## Screenshot 4: "Multi-turn Conversation"
- Show 2-3 messages in the chat (user question, AI answer, follow-up)
- **Caption**: "Have multi-turn conversations with full context awareness"

## Screenshot 5: "Text Applied"
- Show the document with text that was just replaced by an AI suggestion
- Show "Applied to document" confirmation in the panel
- **Caption**: "Approved suggestions are applied directly to your Word document"

---

## How to Capture

### Windows
1. Open Word with the plugin loaded
2. Set up the scene as described above
3. Press `Win + Shift + S` to open Snip & Sketch
4. Select the full Word window (1366x768)
5. Save as PNG

### Mac
1. Press `Cmd + Shift + 4`, then `Space` to capture the window
2. Click the Word window
3. Resize to 1366x768 if needed

### Tips
- Use a real legal document (contract, terms, etc.) for authenticity
- Make sure the AI:ssociate branding is visible in the panel header
- Crop to exactly 1366x768 — Partner Center will reject other sizes
- No personal/sensitive information in the document text
