## Long Description (for the store page)

```
AI:ssociate brings AI-powered legal analysis directly into Microsoft Word.

HOW IT WORKS

1. Highlight any text in your Word document — a clause, paragraph, or entire section.
2. Open the AI:ssociate panel from the Home tab.
3. Ask a question or request a rewrite in the chat sidebar.
4. Review the AI response with legal analysis, risk identification, or suggested rewrites.
5. Click "Apply" to replace the selected text with the suggestion, or "Dismiss" to skip.

WHAT YOU CAN DO

- Analyze contracts: Identify risks, obligations, key terms, and potential issues in any legal text.
- Get legal research: Ask questions about Austrian law across civil, criminal, tax, and public law areas.
- Rewrite clauses: Request simplified, more precise, or alternative wording for any clause.
- Jurisdiction filtering: Filter responses by legal area and Austrian federal state (Bundesland).
- Conversational context: Follow-up questions remember the conversation context for deeper analysis.

BUILT FOR LEGAL PROFESSIONALS

AI:ssociate is designed specifically for lawyers, legal departments, notaries, and law firms working with Austrian and EU law. The AI understands legal terminology, case law references, and regulatory frameworks.

KEY FEATURES

- Chat-style interface in the Word sidebar
- One-click apply: approved suggestions replace the selected text directly
- Streaming responses for fast feedback
- Legal area filter: Civil, Criminal, Tax, Public
- Jurisdiction scope: Bund, Wien, Steiermark, Tirol, and all Austrian states
- Conversation threading for multi-turn analysis
- Works with Word Desktop (Windows and Mac) and Word Online

PRIVACY AND SECURITY

Your document text is sent to the AI:ssociate API only when you explicitly ask a question. No data is stored permanently. See our privacy policy for details.
```
