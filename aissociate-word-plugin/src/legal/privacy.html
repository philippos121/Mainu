AI:ssociate — Privacy Policy

Last updated: April 2026

This privacy policy explains how the AI:ssociate Word Plugin ("the Plugin") collects, uses, and protects your information.

1. What Data We Process

The Plugin processes the following data only when you explicitly submit a question:

  • Selected text: The text you highlight in your Word document and include with your question.
  • Your question: The text you type in the chat input field.
  • Filter selections: Your chosen legal area and jurisdiction scope (if any).
  • Conversation thread ID: A session identifier to maintain conversation context.

2. How Data Is Transmitted

When you click "Send" or press Enter, the selected text and your question are sent via HTTPS (encrypted) to the AI:ssociate API at aissociate.at for processing. No data is sent automatically — transmission only occurs when you explicitly submit a question.

3. What We Do NOT Collect

  • We do not read your entire document — only the text you explicitly select and submit.
  • We do not collect personal information (name, email, IP address) through the Plugin.
  • We do not store your document text permanently on our servers.
  • We do not use your data to train AI models.
  • We do not share your data with third parties.
  • We do not use cookies or tracking technologies in the Plugin.

4. Local Storage

The Plugin stores the following data locally in your browser's localStorage (on your device only):

  • Your API key (encrypted by your browser, never transmitted to us)
  • Your language preference

This data never leaves your device. You can clear it at any time through your browser settings.

5. AI:ssociate API

The AI:ssociate API is operated by AI:ssociate (aissociate.at). When you submit a question, the API processes your request and returns a response. For the AI:ssociate API's own data handling practices, please refer to the AI:ssociate terms at aissociate.at.

6. Data Security

All communication between the Plugin and the AI:ssociate API uses HTTPS encryption. Your API key is stored only on your local device.

7. Your Rights

Under GDPR and Austrian data protection law, you have the right to:

  • Know what data is processed (described in this policy)
  • Stop data processing at any time (simply stop using the Plugin)
  • Delete local data (clear browser localStorage)
  • Request deletion of any server-side data by contacting us

8. Children's Privacy

The Plugin is designed for legal professionals and is not intended for use by children under 16.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this privacy policy from time to time. Changes will be reflected by updating the "Last updated" date at the top of this page.

10. Contact

For privacy-related questions, contact us at: sales@aissociate.at
